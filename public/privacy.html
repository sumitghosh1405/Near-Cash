Near Cash Privacy

Near Cash uses account information needed to operate the service, including a display name and, when enabled, approximate location for nearby matching. Operational and anonymous product analytics may be collected to improve reliability and product flows.

Your controls

Signed-in users can request a data export and can remove saved location data or permanently delete their account through the authenticated privacy controls. Deletion is intended to remove account-associated operational data; limited safety records may require separate retention rules.

Important

This page is a product privacy notice template and should be reviewed and finalized for the actual launch jurisdiction, data flows, vendors, retention periods, and legal requirements.
